Questions

Verify before you reply.

If anything in an email feels inconsistent, pause and confirm it through v5med.net rather than replying to the original message.

Why did I receive email from 12888.de instead of v5med.net?

12888.de is a dedicated outbound business communication domain used by the V5 Medical international business team. Our primary company and product website remains v5med.net.

How can I verify Rex or Leo?

Check that the address is exactly rex@12888.de or leo@12888.de. For independent confirmation, forward the message and its headers to sales@v5med.net.

Where should I submit an RFQ or product specification?

Use the official inquiry page at v5med.net/contact. This keeps your request connected to the current product and privacy workflow.

Will payment details ever change by email?

Do not rely on a single email for a beneficiary or bank-detail change. Verify any sensitive commercial change through an independently sourced contact channel before taking action.

Have a specification or sourcing brief?

Send it through the official V5 Medical inquiry channel and our team will route it to the right product and quality owner.
